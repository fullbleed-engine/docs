# Help keep Fullbleed moving.

Start by using it. Try a real document in the [browser playground](playground.md),
build something from the [examples](examples.md), or bring it into your Python
application. Knowing where Fullbleed helps, and where it gets in your way, is
valuable feedback.

## Support the maintenance

If Fullbleed is useful to you or your team, an optional one-time contribution or
monthly sponsorship helps fund maintainer time, release testing, documentation,
and domain and hosting costs. Fullbleed remains MIT licensed and free to use,
including commercial use.

[Sponsor Fullbleed on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/krflol){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

Sponsorships go to Fullbleed's maintainer, **krflol**. Choose a one-time or monthly
amount on GitHub. Sponsorship does not include private features or a support SLA.

## Other ways to help

- [Report a reproducible issue](https://github.com/fullbleed-engine/fullbleed-official/issues/new/choose) with a small HTML/CSS example and your runtime version.
- [Improve a guide or share a template](https://github.com/fullbleed-engine/fullbleed-official/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md).
- [Show what you built or ask a question](https://github.com/fullbleed-engine/fullbleed-official/discussions).
- Tell another developer about Fullbleed when it fits their document workflow.

Follow the [release notes](https://github.com/fullbleed-engine/fullbleed-official/releases)
to see what is shipping. Thank you for helping make the project useful.
