# Support Fullbleed

If Fullbleed is useful to you or your team, help keep it maintained with a
monthly sponsorship or a one-time contribution.

[Sponsor monthly](https://github.com/sponsors/krflol?frequency=recurring){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[Give once](https://github.com/sponsors/krflol?frequency=one-time){ .md-button }

Choose your amount on GitHub Sponsors. You'll need to sign in to GitHub to
complete checkout. Contributions go to Fullbleed's maintainer,
[Keenan Finkelstein (krflol)](https://github.com/krflol).

## What your support funds

- Maintainer time for engine fixes and improvements.
- Release testing across supported platforms.
- Documentation, examples, and project hosting.

Fullbleed remains MIT licensed and free to use, including commercial use.
Sponsorship does not include private features or a support SLA.

Follow the [release notes](https://github.com/fullbleed-engine/fullbleed-official/releases)
to see what is shipping.

## Other ways to help

Financial support is optional. You can also:

- [Report a reproducible issue](https://github.com/fullbleed-engine/fullbleed-official/issues/new/choose) with a small HTML/CSS example and your runtime version.
- [Improve a guide or share a template](https://github.com/fullbleed-engine/fullbleed-official/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md).
- [Show what you built or ask a question](https://github.com/fullbleed-engine/fullbleed-official/discussions).
