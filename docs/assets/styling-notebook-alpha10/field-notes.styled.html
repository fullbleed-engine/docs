A useful lesson

Make the first step visible. A welcome at the gate, a clearly marked tool bench, and a task that takes ten minutes helped people begin.

Visits and participation counts can include the same neighbor more than once. They describe activity, not unique attendance.

02 / What comes next

Keep the welcome growing.

Three modest commitments will guide the next season. Each has a clear owner and a way for someone new to help.

01

Make room at the table

Reserve a few places at every shared meal for neighbors joining for the first time. Publish the menu and access information together.

02

Pass on a practical skill

Pair each repair session with a short demonstration. Keep a simple printed guide beside the tools for people who prefer to follow at their own pace.

03

Listen before adding more

Ask what made participation easy and what got in the way. Use those answers to choose the next opening hours and activities.
